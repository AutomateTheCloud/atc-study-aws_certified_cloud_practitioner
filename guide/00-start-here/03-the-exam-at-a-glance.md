# The CLF-C02 exam at a glance

## What the exam tests, and where this guide covers it

The exam has four **domains** (subject areas), each with a weight: its share of the scored questions. Each domain lists **task statements**, the specific things it tests: 19 in all.

| Domain | Weight | About how many of the 50 scored questions | Modules that teach it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Cloud Concepts | 24% | 12 | [02 Why the cloud](../02-why-the-cloud/00-brief.md), [13 Moving to AWS](../13-moving-to-aws/00-brief.md) |
| 2 Security and Compliance | 30% | 15 | [04 Who secures what](../04-shared-responsibility-and-access/00-brief.md), [09 Protecting and governing](../09-protecting-and-governing/00-brief.md) |
| 3 Cloud Technology and Services | 34% | 17 | [03 Around the world](../03-aws-around-the-world/00-brief.md), [05 Storing data](../05-storing-data/00-brief.md), [06 Running applications](../06-running-applications/00-brief.md), [07 Connecting it](../07-connecting-it/00-brief.md), [08 Building and operating](../08-building-and-operating/00-brief.md), [10 Data, AI and more](../10-data-ai-and-more/00-brief.md) |
| 4 Billing, Pricing, and Support | 12% | 6 | [11 Paying for AWS](../11-paying-for-aws/00-brief.md), [12 Getting help](../12-getting-help/00-brief.md) |

![Horizontal bar chart of the four domain weights: Domain 1 Cloud Concepts 24%, about 12 scored questions; Domain 2 Security and Compliance 30%, about 15; Domain 3 Cloud Technology and Services 34%, about 17; Domain 4 Billing, Pricing, and Support 12%, about 6.](../../images/00-03-domain-weights.svg)

*Notice that Domains 2 and 3 together are 64% of the exam. Domain 3 lists the most services, but each of its eight task statements gets only about two questions. Question counts are our arithmetic (weight × 50 scored questions), not AWS's figures; each exam form varies.*

The [blueprint map](../99-reference/06-blueprint-map.md) lists every page that teaches each of the 19 tasks, and both practice exams cover all of them.

## What the exam leaves out

AWS's exam guide lists what the target candidate isn't expected to do. None of it is tested:

| Not tested | Source |
|---|---|
| Writing code | [Exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/cloud-practitioner-02/cloud-practitioner-02.html), target candidate |
| Designing cloud architecture | Same |
| Troubleshooting | Same |
| Implementation | Same |
| Load and performance testing | Same |

So this guide teaches what each service is for and how it differs from the one it's confused with, not how to configure it; each module's Beyond the exam page lists what it left out. The exam guide also lists **out-of-scope services**, such as AWS CodeDeploy, AWS CloudShell, AWS Transfer Family and AWS Network Firewall. They appear only as wrong answers.

## Format, scoring, booking and retakes

| Item | As of 2026-10-23 |
|---|---|
| Questions | 65: 50 scored and 15 **unscored** (new questions AWS is trying out). You can't tell which are which, so answer every one ([exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/cloud-practitioner-02/cloud-practitioner-02.html)) |
| Question types | **Multiple choice:** one correct answer out of four. **Multiple response:** two or more correct out of five or more. No hands-on tasks |
| Time | 90 minutes; non-native English speakers taking it in English can request 30 more (ESL +30), once, before registering ([before testing](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/before-testing/)) |
| Scoring | Pass or fail on a **scaled score** from 100 to 1,000 (adjusted so every version is equally hard to pass); **700** passes. **Compensatory:** you pass on your total, not domain by domain. A blank counts as wrong; a wrong guess costs nothing |
| Price and booking | USD 100 per attempt, through an AWS Certification Account separate from your lab account. Free changes up to 24 hours before, at most two reschedules; no changes or refunds within 24 hours |
| Delivery | A Pearson VUE test center, or online with a remote proctor and no breaks ([during testing](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/during-testing/)); twelve languages, with Italian and German retiring after 2026-12-31; no prerequisites ([exam page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-cloud-practitioner/)) |
| Results, retakes and validity | Results within 5 business days. If you fail, wait 14 days and pay again; if you pass, you can't retake it for 2 years. The certification lasts 3 years ([after testing](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/after-testing/)) |

## When the exam changes, and confidentiality

AWS revised the exam guide in 2026 (support plan names, some service names, some services removed) and kept the code CLF-C02; learn both old and new names ([AWS changes fast](04-aws-changes-fast.md)). Revisions are published at least a month before the exam changes, and a new service must be generally available for 3 months before it can appear ([Certification FAQs](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/faqs/)).

Under the [AWS Certification Program Agreement](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certification-agreement/) you keep exam questions confidential. Using questions shared without AWS's permission, such as "real exam questions" posted online, breaks it, and AWS can revoke a certification even after issuing it. Every question in this guide is our own, written from the public exam guide.
